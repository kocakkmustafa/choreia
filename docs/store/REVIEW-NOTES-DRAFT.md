## English — proposed reviewer notes

Choreia contains five solo dance study paths with 86 bundled written units:
HipHop Foundations (6 foundation + 8 skill units), Salsa Shines (48 solo
Salsa On1 lessons), Line Dance (9), Latin Fusion Fitness (8) and original
short-form combinations (7). These are study records and self-checks, not
certification of dance skill. Demonstration videos and an in-app music
catalogue are not included in this candidate.

1. Complete the first-run introduction. Continue without an account.
2. Open STYLES and choose a path. Open a written unit, read its task/count
   structure, and record your own practice. Revisit the unit and the PROGRESS
   tab. Records stay on this device within the current guest or account scope.
3. PROFILE → Account opens the optional shared Motion sign-in/registration
   flow. Signing in is not required for bundled lessons. PROFILE also exposes
   the open-source licenses. The account screen includes account deletion;
   deletion affects the shared Motion identity and requires explicit confirmation.
4. Live camera practice, subscription purchases and restoration entry are
   closed in this release policy. No purchase or camera permission is needed
   to review the bundled written paths. No physical movement, step identity,
   music alignment or mastery grade is claimed.

No reviewer credentials are embedded in this document. Before submission,
complete real account/device acceptance and provide any credentials or access
instructions required for review of the optional account features through the
store's private review fields. Do not claim these checks were completed by
host tests. No fake account or paid entitlement should be supplied.
